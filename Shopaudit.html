Stock, sales & audit for your shops
  • We don't have your password, so nobody on our side can sign in to your account.
  • We make our money from subscriptions, not from your shop's numbers. Your sales and profit aren't worth anything to us.
  • If someone wanted to know what you sell, they could just walk into your shop and look. Nobody needs to register you, give you a free trial, and set up your whole inventory just to find out you stock rice and oil.
  • We run many shops on this platform. Our job is keeping it running, not watching any one shop's profit.
  • We don't process your shop's sales or hold your shop's money. The only money that ever moves through TheMonishop is your own subscription fee.
Only pay your subscription inside the app. Never pay cash or transfer to a marketer or anyone claiming to represent TheMonishop. A payment made outside the app is at your own risk.
