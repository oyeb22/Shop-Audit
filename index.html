Questions

Before you get started

How does it stop staff theft?

Every sale and stock change is tied to the person who made it, and item edits need your approval before they apply. When you reconcile physical stock against what the system expects, any shortage shows up immediately as a logged variance, not a mystery at month end.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Everything is stored in a secured database with row-level access control. Only people you have approved into your shop can see your data, and each role sees only what it needs to.

Does it work without internet?

No. TheMonishop is cloud-synced across your devices so your team always sees the same numbers, which means it needs an internet connection to save and load data.

Can my staff use it too?

Yes. Share your shop's invite code. New members land as pending until you approve them and pick their role, Manager or Receptionist.

What does the paid plan cover?

It removes the 15-item limit and unlocks team accounts for a year from the day you pay. Renew at the same flat ₦20,000 whenever it's due. There's a grace period after expiry, so you won't lose access without warning.

How do I get started?

Create a free account, add your items, and start selling. Upgrade only when you outgrow 15 items or need to add your team.
