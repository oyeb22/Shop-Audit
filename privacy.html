Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

This describes what information TheMonishop collects when you use the service, and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password, used to sign in.
  • Business data you enter: items, prices, stock levels, sales, expenses, and customer records (names, phone numbers, credit balances) that you or your team add while using the app.
  • Payment records: when you upgrade, Paystack processes the payment directly. We store only the transaction reference and amount to confirm your business was upgraded. We never see or store your card details.
  • Marketer applications: if you apply to our Marketer Program, we collect your full name, phone number, WhatsApp number, and email address.
  • Marketer payout details: if approved, the bank name, account number and account name you provide so bonuses can be paid to you.

How we use it

Your data is used only to provide the service to you and your team: showing your stock and sales, calculating profit, sending the WhatsApp messages you choose to send, and keeping the audit log that shows who did what.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

WhatsApp reminders

Reminder, thank-you and custom messages are sent by opening WhatsApp on your own device with the message pre-filled. TheMonishop does not send these messages on your behalf and does not have access to your WhatsApp account.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase, our database and authentication provider, stores your data securely with access controls so only your approved team members can see it.
  • Paystack processes payments when you upgrade. Their handling of your payment details is governed by Paystack's own privacy policy.

We do not share your business data with any other third party.

Marketer application and payout details are visible only to platform administrators reviewing applications and processing bonus payouts — not to shop owners, other marketers, or any third party.

Local storage

The app uses your browser's local storage to remember small things like which customers you have already sent a daily WhatsApp round to. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your business's data, or request that it be deleted, by contacting us. If you delete your account, your business data is removed unless another admin on the same business needs it retained.

Children

TheMonishop is a business tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and change the date above.

Contact

Questions about your data can be sent to hello@themonishop.com.ng or via WhatsApp at 0808 833 4592.
